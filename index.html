Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose a PC MikroTik Router over a Cloud Core Router (CCR)? ▼

PC MikroTik routers utilize high-frequency Intel Xeon multi-core processors, massive DDR4 ECC RAM, and dedicated PCIe 10G/40G NICs. For heavy Single-Thread queues, intense BGP convergence, and large PPPoE/CGNAT state tables, custom PC MikroTik builds provide up to 3x higher throughput at a fraction of the hardware cost compared to CCR units.
